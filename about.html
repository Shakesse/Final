Lash education is the foundation of every set.If you are an Ohio licensed cosmetologist or esthetician we are the studio to learn from.

Our program covers eye anatomy, natural lash growth cycles,isolation techniques,lash adhesive chemistry and Ohio's policies on sanitation.

Our artists take so much pride in our work. We have consultations before every appointment, map lashes to compliment all eye shapes to assure the right curl,length and diameter to not cause any lash damage.

Due to the popularity of the lash clusters and viral TikToks, our salon chooses to stay teachable.Continuing Education is a must. Our staff stays current on the latest and advanced techniques.
